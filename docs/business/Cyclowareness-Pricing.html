Questions

Good to know

Which languages are included?

The learner app, the admin console and the training library are available in ten languages: Azerbaijani, English, Russian, Turkish, Georgian, Ukrainian, Romanian, German, French and Italian. Employees choose their own language. Other languages, such as Arabic, can be added on request and are quoted separately.

What happens when an employee reports something suspicious?

The email, file or link goes to the sandbox, where it is analysed without being opened or run. The case is then routed to your SOC or responsible security team, who confirm the verdict, respond, and let the employee know the outcome.

How do threat-informed labs work?

When an APT campaign is publicly disclosed, or organizations similar to yours are attacked, our team analyses the attack and rebuilds it as a safe hands-on lab and a short lesson, so employees practise against what attackers are doing now.

What does the AI tutor send to the AI model?

Only the lesson content and the learner's question. Names, email addresses, scores and other personal data are never sent. AI-generated courses are saved as drafts and are published only by a person on your team.

How are SMS simulations delivered?

Through a carrier or SMS gateway, either ours or one you already use, connected during onboarding. Carrier charges are passed on at cost. The per-message range is listed under Additional costs.

Where does the platform run?

In our cloud by default, in a dedicated cloud instance, or on your own servers. The deployment option, support level and volume pricing are agreed in the contract.
