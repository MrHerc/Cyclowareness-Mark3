# Cyclowareness: learner quick start

One page for employees. Your organisation chooses which of these features it uses, so some may be missing from your menu. The app follows the language set in your profile; you can switch it with the language picker.

## 1. Sign in

- Go to your organisation's Cyclowareness address and sign in with your **work email**.
- Depending on your organisation, you then enter your password, continue through your organisation's own sign-in (single sign-on), or choose **Sign in with a phone number** and enter the code sent by text message.
- Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
- A short tour shows where everything is. Profile → **Show the tour again** brings it back.

## 2. Find your way

| Menu | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your next lesson (the Continue card), your learning path, your department's place in the league, policies to sign and open risks |
| **Learn** | The catalogue of short modules |
| **Paths** | The modules chosen for you, in order, and the practice labs that open after their lessons |
| **Lab** | Short interactive drills. Safe: they touch no real system, and the result adds training XP without changing your risk score |
| **League** | This month's department league. Finishing modules and reporting phishing moves your department up |
| **Progress** | Your level, XP, streak, risk score and every module you started |
| **My security** | Your risk score, the risks your own actions created, what you reported, and the sandbox for checking a link or file |
| **Live sessions** | Short talks and Q&A with the security team; register for a place |
| **Help** | Ask the support desk, or rate the platform and share an idea (anonymously if you wish) |
| **Profile** | Certificates, policies, language, sound, motion, the question timer, security and your data |

## 3. Learn

- A lesson takes about 3 minutes. AI Sel, the cat, reads it aloud and highlights the words as it reads.
- In the player: play or pause, volume, speed (0.75× to 2×), subtitles, the lesson transcript, full screen, and the contents panel with the lesson's parts. Keyboard: **Space** play or pause, **arrows** previous and next part, **M** sound, **C** subtitles, **T** transcript, **S** contents, **?** all shortcuts. The letter shortcuts can be turned off if you use speech input.
- Some questions in medium and hard modules have a timer. Profile → **Question timer** gives you more time (60 seconds instead of 30) or turns it off.
- Where your organisation has the AI tutor, you can ask AI Sel a question about the lesson.
- After a module's last lesson, **confirm the module** with the statement your organisation set. Only then does it count as completed, and your **certificate** appears in Profile. Each certificate has a serial number and a verification link you can share.

## 4. Report something suspicious

A strange email, text, call, link or file? Use the **Report** button at the top of the app (also on My security and Help). Say where it came from and what happened; the security team checks every report, and each report earns XP. Do not forward the message or open its attachments.

The organisation also sends **practice phishing** (simulations). If you click one, you see a short explanation of what gave it away and a training is assigned to you. Reporting it is the right move.

**Not sure about a link or file?** My security → **Sandbox** checks it safely before you open it. It is static analysis: the file is not run or stored (only its fingerprint and the findings are kept), and links are not opened.

## 5. XP, streaks and risk

- Lessons and reports earn **XP** towards your daily goal and your level.
- Learning on consecutive days keeps your **streak**. One lesson a day is enough. A **freeze** covers one missed day; you earn one for every 7 days in a row, up to 2.
- Your **risk score** comes from unfinished and overdue training, risky actions in simulations, open risks and how often you answer right on the first try. Lower is better. An open risk closes when you finish the training linked to it.

## 6. Policies

Policies to sign are on Home and in Profile → Policies. Read the text, tick "I have read this policy, I understand it and I will follow it" and sign. When a new version is published, you are asked to sign again.

## 7. Your account

Profile → **Security**:

- Turn on **two-step verification** with an authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator), and keep the one-time backup codes shown at setup. Some organisations require it.
- **Change password** (your other devices are signed out) and **Sign out other devices**.

Profile → **Your data**:

- **Download my data:** your profile and learning history in one file.
- **Request account deletion:** your administrator reviews it. After approval your name, email, photo and sign-in data are removed; learning results stay without your name.

Profile → **Motion** sets how much the app animates (Full, Calm or Still), and **Sound and vibration** whether AI Sel reads aloud.

Something not working? Profile → **Report a problem**, or the problem button in the lesson player.
