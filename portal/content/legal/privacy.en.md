# Privacy Policy

What personal data is processed on the platform, for what purpose and on what legal basis, who is responsible, what is sent to AI Sel, where and for how long data is stored, how it is protected and how you can exercise your rights.

## 1. About This Policy

1.1. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed in the Cyclowareness service (hereinafter, the "Service"), an awareness platform for cyber hygiene and information security provided by [to be provided] (registration number [to be provided], address [to be provided]; hereinafter, the "Provider").
1.2. The Service is used by organisations that open accounts for their employees and other persons they have authorised (each, hereinafter, an "Organisation"). In this Policy, "you" means a person who has an account in the Service.
1.3. This Policy has been prepared in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan "On Personal Data" and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation of the European Union (GDPR), the UK GDPR and Law No. 6698 of the Republic of Türkiye on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). ## 2. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

2.1. Your Organisation determines for what purpose and how your data is used in the Service: it registers you, assigns training and sees your results. Under Azerbaijani legislation, it is the owner of the personal data; under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the KVKK, it is the controller.
2.2. The Provider operates the Service on behalf of the Organisation and processes your data only on its instructions, under an agreement containing personal data protection obligations (an agreement on the processing of personal data). Under Azerbaijani legislation, the Provider is the operator to which the owner has entrusted the processing (Article 9.9 of the Law "On Personal Data"); under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the KVKK, it is the processor.
2.3. The Provider is itself responsible as owner (controller) only for the data that it uses for its own purposes: the accounts of its own staff who administer the platform, the protection of the platform as a whole against attacks and abuse, and correspondence sent directly to the Provider.
2.4. It is advisable to send requests concerning how your Organisation uses your data to the Organisation. The Provider forwards the requests it receives to the Organisation and helps it respond to them.

## 5. AI Sel (AI Assistant)

5.1. When you ask AI Sel (the AI assistant) a question, the Service sends only the question, the text of the current lesson and the interface language to the Anthropic API (Anthropic PBC, United States), which generates the answer. Your name, email address, job title and the name of your Organisation are never sent. Subtitles translated by AI and lesson drafts in the training studio are produced by the same service from the lesson text and, for drafts, from the topic entered by the content author; they contain no data about learners.
5.2. The Service does not filter or mask the question: it is sent as you typed it or as your browser converted it into text. Do not include personal data, passwords or confidential information in your questions. AI Sel is instructed never to ask for or repeat passwords, codes or personal data.
5.3. A spoken question is converted into text by the speech recognition built into your browser. Which company provides that speech engine depends on the browser; some browsers send the recording to their maker's servers for this purpose, under that maker's terms. The Service receives only the resulting text, never the recording.
5.4. So that the same question can be answered again without sending a new request, the question text and the answer are stored with the lesson and your Organisation, without any link to your account. AI Sel's answer, and any lesson sentence without a pre-produced narration clip, is read aloud by the speech voice of your browser or device (some browsers use an online voice service for this). The narration of the lessons is produced in advance from the lesson text by a text-to-speech service (currently ElevenLabs, or an open-source engine operated by the Provider); it receives no personal data, and nothing is sent to it while you learn.
5.5. Anthropic processes these requests under its commercial terms, under which it does not use customer data to train its models. Anthropic may retain requests for a limited period to detect abuse, as described in its terms.
5.6. Your Organisation may disable AI Sel or spoken questions.

## 6. Who Can See Your Data

6.1. Within your Organisation: Administrators see your data in accordance with their roles. For example, a department head sees only the departments assigned to them. Other participants do not see your results.
6.2. Systems connected by your Organisation: its mail server (the Service's emails are sent through the Organisation's mail server), its identity provider or directory (for sign-in and employee synchronisation), and SIEM or ITSM systems that may receive security events, including your user identifier, email address and department.
6.3. The Provider's sub-processors (Cloud Version):
- UpCloud (UpCloud Ltd, Finland, and its subsidiaries): servers, file storage and backups in a data centre located in London, United Kingdom;
- Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, United States): the Anthropic API for AI Sel's answers, subtitles translated by AI and lesson drafts in the training studio, in accordance with section 5;
- ElevenLabs (United States): speech synthesis of the lesson narration from the lesson text only, in accordance with clause 5.4; it receives no personal data;
- where your Organisation uses text messages (one-time sign-in codes or simulated SMS attacks): the text-message operator named in the agreement on the processing of personal data, which receives the phone number and the text of the message.
6.4. Public certificate page: where your Organisation enables public verification, anyone who has the link to your certificate sees your name, the module, the date, the Organisation and the serial number. Nothing else about you is shown.
6.5. State bodies: only where required by legislation and within the limits it sets.
6.6. The server monitoring notifications used by the Provider do not contain personal data.
6.7. The Provider informs Organisations in advance of a new sub-processor, as set out in the agreement on the processing of personal data.

## 7. Where Data Is Stored and Cross-Border Transfer

7.1. In the Cloud Version, data is stored on servers located in London, United Kingdom. For Organisations in Azerbaijan, this is a cross-border transfer within the meaning of Article 14 of the Law "On Personal Data". The United Kingdom, like Azerbaijan, is a party to the Council of Europe Convention for the Protection of Individuals with regard to Automatic Processing of Personal Data (Convention 108) and protects personal data under the UK GDPR. The European Commission has recognised that the United Kingdom ensures an adequate level of protection; that decision was renewed in December 2025.
7.2. Requests to Anthropic are processed in the United States. They do not contain names, email addresses or job titles (section 5). Processing by Anthropic is governed by its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for transfers outside the European Union. ElevenLabs, also in the United States, receives only lesson text and no personal data.
7.3. If your Organisation is required to store data in a particular country, it may use the Local Version or agree on a different hosting location in its agreement.

## 8. How Long Data Is Kept

- Account data: for as long as the account exists. When an account is deleted, the name, email address, picture, job title, sign-in data, sessions, and the IP addresses of attestations, of familiarisation with policies and of QR scans are deleted immediately; so that the Organisation's statistics and evidence remain accurate, the training history, attestations, certificates and audit records are retained under an anonymous marker.
- Sign-in session: ends after 8 hours of inactivity, unless a different value is set in the Organisation's installation.
- Two-step sign-in and single sign-on steps: 10 minutes. Download links: 5 minutes.
- AI Sel usage counters: 24 hours.
- AI Sel's stored questions and answers (without a link to any account): for as long as your Organisation's account in the Service exists.
- Demo requests from the public website: 24 months, unless deleted sooner (clause 3.3).
- Email delivery records: 90 days after delivery is completed.
- Directory synchronisation reports: 180 days.
- Audit log: cannot be changed or deleted through the Service; it is kept for as long as the Organisation's agreement is in force and, thereafter, for the period set out in the agreement or required by legislation.
- Backups (Cloud Version): replaced by new ones on a continuous rolling cycle, currently 14 days.
- After termination of the agreement: the Organisation's data is returned or deleted as set out in its agreement; backups expire in accordance with their cycle.
8.1. Data is not kept longer than the purpose requires (Article 9.4 of the Law "On Personal Data"; Article 5(1)(e) of the GDPR). Your Organisation may set shorter periods in its agreement.

## 9. How Data Is Protected

- all connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS);
- passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; the passwords of connected mail servers, single sign-on keys and integration keys are encrypted;
- each Organisation's data is separated at database level by row-level security, so one Organisation cannot see another Organisation's data;
- two-step sign-in, which the Organisation can make mandatory for Administrators, limits on the number of sign-in attempts, and device limits;
- role-based access: each Administrator sees only what their role and scope of authority permit;
- an append-only audit log of sign-ins and administrative actions;
- in the Cloud Version: daily backups, hardened servers that permit administrative access only with a key, and regular updates;
- the Provider's staff who have access to personal data are bound, including after they leave employment, by an obligation not to disclose the data (Article 5.5 of the Law "On Personal Data").
9.1. In the event of a personal data breach, the Provider informs the Organisation concerned without undue delay and provides it with the information it needs to meet its own obligations, including, where applicable, the 72-hour deadline under Article 33 of the GDPR.

## 10. Your Rights

10.1. Under Article 7 of the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan "On Personal Data", you have the following rights:
- to know whether data about you is being processed, and who its owner and operator are;
- to know the legal basis, purpose, period and methods of processing, and the persons who have the right to access your data;
- to access the content of your data and to learn its sources;
- to demand that your data be rectified and, except in cases established by legislation, destroyed;
- to demand that the collection and processing of your data be prohibited, and to object to them, except where processing is mandatory under legislation;
- to object to a decision that is taken by means of information technology and infringes your interests;
- to file a complaint with the competent executive authority in the field of personal data or with a court, and to demand compensation for damage caused.
10.2. Where the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the KVKK applies, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country (in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).
10.3. How you can exercise your rights:
- in the Service: under Profile, Your data, you can download your data as a file and request the deletion of your account; your Organisation's Administrator approves or rejects the request and, if rejecting it, must state the reason;
- through your Organisation, which is responsible for your data;
- by writing to the address [to be provided]; the Provider forwards the request to your Organisation and helps it respond.
10.4. Under Article 7.5 of the Law "On Personal Data", a written application on paper submitted with an identity document, or an electronic request signed with an enhanced electronic signature, is always accepted. The tools in the Service are an additional and simpler route.
10.5. Under Azerbaijani legislation, a request is answered free of charge within 7 working days; where it is necessary to contact a third party, this period may be extended by a further 7 working days; in the event of a refusal, a reasoned response is given within 5 working days (Article 12 of the Law "On Personal Data"). Under the GDPR, the period is one month. The Provider helps the Organisation comply with these periods.
10.6. Some records, such as evidence of a completed attestation or of familiarisation with a policy, cannot be deleted for as long as a legal reason to keep them remains. Such records are kept without your name, as described in section 8.
