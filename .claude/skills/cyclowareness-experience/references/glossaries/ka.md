## Style rules
REGISTER: formal თქვენ throughout, 2nd person plural verb forms (შეამოწმეთ, დააჭირეთ, შეატყობინეთ, გადარეკეთ). Mkhedruli has no capitals, so formality comes from the verb forms alone. Prohibitions: არ + optative in instructions (არ გახსნათ, არ გადარიცხოთ); "never" is always არასოდეს + optative (არასოდეს ენდოთ, არასოდეს გადაუხადოთ); ნუ + future (ნუ გადაუხდით) only in a short slogan with no "never" in the source. AI Sel speaks warmly and plainly, in short spoken sentences that sound good aloud; no bureaucratic chains of genitives. Titles: short noun phrases or imperatives, no final full stop (except a quoted "Stop." punchline, as in the fake-payment-links subtitle). Word order for "fake/risky X of Y": genitive + adjective + noun (შესვლის ყალბი ფანჯრები, ბრაუზერის სარისკო გაფართოებები), so the adjective clearly qualifies the head noun.
TERM CLASH: შეტყობინება means both "message/notification" and "report". Use it for SMS/chat messages; for reporting prefer the verb (შეატყობინეთ, შეატყობინა) or "...-ის შესახებ შეტყობინება". Never write a sentence where one შეტყობინება is the message and the next is the report.
DATA: fixed terms keep the formal genitive მონაცემთა (მონაცემთა გაჟონვა, პერსონალურ მონაცემთა დაცვა); everywhere else მონაცემების (მონაცემების გაზიარება, მონაცემების მომპარავი პროგრამა).
GENDER: Georgian has no grammatical gender. Female surnames keep -ova/-eva (ალიევა, declined ალიევას/ალიევას), male -ovi/-evi (ალიევი, ალიევს/ალიევის). For a generic person: ადამიანი, თანამშრომელი, კოლეგა.
QUOTES: „…“ (U+201E opening, U+201C closing), also for UI button names quoted in text: დააჭირეთ „დაშვებას“. Nested quotes: '…'. Never «» or straight "" in visible text (straight quotes only as JSON syntax).
PUNCTUATION: comma, full stop, ? and ! with no space before them. Dash: en dash with spaces ( – ). Comma before subordinate clauses with რომ/როცა/თუ/ვინც/რომელიც/სადაც (იცოდეთ, ვის შეუძლია…). No comma before და in a list. Colon as in the source.
LATIN ABBREVIATIONS AND NUMBERS take Georgian endings after a hyphen: SMS-ით, SMS-ები, QR-კოდი, PIN-კოდი, SIM-ის, Wi-Fi-ზე, VPN-ით, USB-ფლეშკა, IT-ის, AI Sel-ს, AI Sel-ის, 15-ში, 2027-ში. As a plain modifier before a Georgian noun, no hyphen: IT მხარდაჭერა, AI დამხმარე. Ordinals: მე-3, მე-15.
NUMBERS: decimal comma (12,5); thousands separator a no-break space (1 000; 25 000 AZN); percent with no space (15%). A bare decimal value the checker protects (12.5) stays as it is.
CURRENCY: amounts and the code AZN stay as in the source; the word manat becomes მანათი (250 მანათი; the noun stays singular after a number). Never convert to GEL/ლარი.
DATES AND TIMES: numeric 27.10.2026; in words 2026 წლის 27 ოქტომბერი (short: 27 ოქტომბერი); month and weekday names lower case; 24-hour time 09:12 unchanged.
SCRIPT: Mkhedruli only (U+10D0–U+10FA). Never Mtavruli (U+1C90–U+1CBF) in running text; never CSS text-transform: uppercase on Georgian strings (it renders Mtavruli). Font must cover Georgian (e.g. Noto Sans Georgian). Never transliterate Georgian into Latin. No Latin letters inside Georgian words.
NEVER TRANSLATE (byte-for-byte): Cyclowareness; Safarov Industries; AI Sel; fictional brand and product names (NeyroSöhbət, İşPoçt, BuludXidmət, SahilBank, ManatPay, Nordvale, Nordhaven Clinic, Cloudora, Kontakto, ParolSeyf, Xəzri, Görüş, Gorush, Alov Servis, Serin Klimat, Lumera Hotel, Khazar Logistics, Nargiz Market, Laman Handmade, etc.) and brandRef ids; e-mail addresses, domains, URLs (also inside a sentence or a "Name <address>" To line); phone numbers, IBANs, card numbers, codes, times, amounts; Latin abbreviations Wi-Fi, VPN, SMS, QR, PIN, SIM, USB, CAPTCHA, MFA, IT, XP, IBAN, HR, Bcc; JSON ids and keys; file names the checker protects (ASCII-only names such as Rekvizitler.pdf, Sened.pdf.exe).
SETTING: stories stay in the fictional Azerbaijani company world; do not relocate them to Georgia. Real places take Georgian exonyms: Baku → ბაქო, Ganja → განჯა, Azerbaijan → აზერბაიჯანი, Narimanov district → ნარიმანოვის რაიონი, Vienna → ვენა, Amsterdam → ამსტერდამი, United Kingdom → გაერთიანებული სამეფო. Fictional agencies stay fictional (translate descriptive parts only, e.g. "Office portal" → ოფისის პორტალი, keeping brandRef).
ARTEFACTS: emails, SMS, chats and calls read as a realistic attack in natural Georgian and keep every red flag. Keep the number and order of spans with spot/link/reveal; each spot still holds exactly its phrase.
TITLES: lesson titles equal i18n.ka.json lessons["module/lesson"]; module names exactly as in i18n.ka.json modules[id][0].

## Character names
RULE: transliterate from the Azerbaijani pronunciation into Mkhedruli, using the forms Georgians already know (Georgian press writes ნიზამედინ გულიევი, ქალაქ განჯა). Letters: k → ქ, t → თ, p → ფ, ç → ჩ, c/j → ჯ, h → ჰ, q → გ, ğ → ღ, x/kh → ხ, ş/sh → შ, ü → იუ, ö → ო, ı → ი, y between vowels → ი, ə → ა or ე as in the established form below (the table wins over the letter rule). Map every English spelling variant to ONE form below; do not copy the source's variance. A first name ending in a consonant stays in its stem form before a surname (ქამრან ალიევი); alone it takes nominative -ი (ქამრანი, თურალი, აისელი). Vowel-final names decline normally (ლეილა, საბინა, ნარმინა). In e-mail addresses and identifiers (e.mammadov@…) names stay unchanged.
